Fares and tickets

  • One ride on the metro, a bus or a minibus costs 1 GEL and includes free transfers for 90 minutes.
  • Pay by tapping a contactless bank card on the reader at the metro gate or inside the bus. No cash on buses. Foreign cards may be charged about 1.50 GEL.
  • Or buy a Metromoney card for 2 GEL at any metro ticket window and top it up.
  • The metro runs from about 06:00 to midnight. Most buses run until about 23:00.
  • Apps: Google Maps shows live Tbilisi transit. Taxis: Bolt and Yandex Go.

From Tbilisi International Airport

Taxi, the easy way

Open Bolt or Yandex Go after landing and set the destination to "Wine House, Saakadze 67". Expect 30 to 40 GEL and 30 to 40 minutes. Airport Wi-Fi sometimes blocks the apps, so use mobile data. A SIM card kiosk in Arrivals is open 24 hours. Politely ignore drivers who approach you inside the hall.

Bus 337, the cheap way

The stop is directly outside Arrivals. Buses run from about 07:00 to 23:00, every 15 to 20 minutes, and take about an hour to Station Square. Fare 1 GEL, tap your bank card. At Station Square, take the metro one stop in the direction of Akhmeteli Theatre to Nadzaladevi and walk six minutes, or take a Bolt for about 5 GEL.

Rustaveli Avenue is closed to traffic from July to November 2026, so bus 337 takes a diversion through the centre. It still ends at Station Square.

Transfer with a driver

Marina can arrange a driver to meet you at Arrivals. Send her your flight number on WhatsApp.

From Kutaisi Airport

Coaches meet every flight and leave about an hour after landing. The trip takes 3.5 to 4.5 hours and costs 25 to 30 GEL. Book online before you fly.

  • Georgian Bus stops behind Tbilisi Central Station, next to Station Square metro. This is the best choice for Wine House: one metro stop or a 5 GEL taxi from there.
  • Omnibus and Metro Georgia end at Ortachala bus station on the other side of town. From there a Bolt costs about 15 GEL.

From the central railway station

  • Metro: enter Station Square, Line 1 in the direction of Akhmeteli Theatre, and get off at the first stop, Nadzaladevi.
  • On foot: 1.8 km, about 22 minutes.
  • Taxi: 5 to 7 GEL.

Back from the centre in the evening

  • Metro from Liberty Square, Rustaveli or Marjanishvili in the direction of Akhmeteli Theatre to Nadzaladevi. The last train leaves the centre shortly before midnight.
  • After midnight a Bolt from the Old Town costs about 10 to 15 GEL.
  • Bus 395 runs between Baratashvili Street at the edge of the Old Town and the Gldani district and stops near the house.

Day trips and onward travel

  • Didube bus station, two metro stops north: minibuses to Mtskheta (20 min), Gori, Kazbegi (3 h), Kutaisi (3.5 h) and Borjomi. Pay the driver in cash.
  • Central Station: trains to Batumi, Kutaisi and Zugdidi, and the overnight train to Yerevan. Tickets at tkt.ge or at the station.
  • Ortachala bus station: international buses to Yerevan and Baku. Bolt about 15 GEL.
  • Kakheti wine region: ask Marina. She can arrange a driver for a wine day.
