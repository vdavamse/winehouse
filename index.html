It starts with Marina

At Wine House, guests arrive as visitors and leave feeling like part of the family. This welcoming guesthouse is a peaceful place to rest away from Tbilisi's busy tourist streets, while Nadzaladevi metro, about 500 metres away, takes you to the city centre in minutes.

What truly makes Wine House special is Marina. Her kindness, generosity and genuine Georgian hospitality make you feel at home from the moment you arrive. Whether you need a local recommendation, help arranging transport or simply a friendly welcome after a day in the city, Marina is always happy to help.

Comfortable rooms, a charming shared courtyard, private parking and shops and restaurants close by complete the experience. Gogito, the house dog, will probably say hello too.
